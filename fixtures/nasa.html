Worlds beyond our Sun

An exoplanet is a planet orbiting a star beyond our solar system. Researchers compare many kinds of evidence: a star's changing brightness, its slight wobble, and the light that passes through a planet's atmosphere.

Reading an atmosphere with Webb

When a planet crosses its star, a small fraction of starlight can filter through the planet's atmosphere. Webb's infrared instruments help scientists study patterns in that light. A spectral feature is a clue to interpret alongside uncertainty, clouds, chemistry, and repeated observations.
